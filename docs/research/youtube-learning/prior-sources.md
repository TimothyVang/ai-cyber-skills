## Adopted: design boundaries, not vendor recipes

- **Ground the operating envelope and trace a real request.** Define journeys, non-goals, traffic, maintenance capacity and cost/availability priorities before choosing components. Trace authority, durable writes, asynchronous execution and delivery, rather than treating a service-icon diagram as a guarantee. Jan's [requirements discussion, 2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5nM5sB_kqYI&t=133s) and the AWS demonstration's [overengineering discussion, 10:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hOYzvUQKq8s&t=632s) motivate the current architect additions. Neither establishes measured scale or a universally preferred stack.
- **Make authority survive time and boundaries.** Separate identity, tenant, permission and paid entitlement; include demotion/removal and open-connection revocation. At [52:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5nM5sB_kqYI&t=3130s), the narration simplifies token refresh. The [pinned permission implementation](https://github.com/ski043/kinde-systems-design/blob/5580a86f26aeabdbc8dada153fc66186e12d4a2f/apps/web/lib/kinde/permissions.ts#L5-L72) additionally falls back to live permissions when claims predate a role change, with a 30-second cache. Immediate global revocation is not established.
- **Validate the actual public representation.** The [API demonstration, 43:03 to 44:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xp4g7k8r1TE&t=2583s), distinguishes TypeScript structural compatibility from runtime removal of extra fields. Check the project's actual serializer/adapters; a compile-time interface does not redact data.
- **Model relationships and deletion together.** The database walkthrough combines a [unique child foreign key, 33:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xq2fYRA3dj8&t=2014s), with [nullability and deletion choices, 52:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xq2fYRA3dj8&t=3122s). Specify cardinality, uniqueness scope, nullability and retention. A unique non-null child FK means at most one child per parent, not that every parent has a child.
- **Separate successful work, usage and quota reservation.** The [billing walkthrough, 1:05:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5nM5sB_kqYI&t=3925s), motivates a business idempotency key, completion definition and reconciliation. But [allowance code](https://github.com/ski043/kinde-systems-design/blob/5580a86f26aeabdbc8dada153fc66186e12d4a2f/apps/web/lib/agent/ai-replies.ts#L20-L58) counts succeeded replies; that check alone is not an atomic concurrent quota reservation. “Exactly once” needs end-to-end evidence, not narration.

## Adopted: bounded operations and informed review

- **Stage live-data changes.** At [36:05 to 37:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hOYzvUQKq8s&t=2165s), the speakers distinguish their one-record demo from a phased, synchronized live-user migration. Define copy/synchronization checks, cutover, downtime and approval points. Retaining an old table is not a tested rollback.
- **Constrain load-test side effects.** A [proposed low-cost run, 1:01:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hOYzvUQKq8s&t=3668s), later produces [unexpected article/storage growth, 1:15:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hOYzvUQKq8s&t=4556s). Declare authorized target, read/write mix, test-data namespace, spend including downstream services, eligibility, stop conditions and cleanup before traffic. Dashboard requests are not a capacity measurement.
- **Verify usable integrations, not just consent.** The [uploader's companion guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bUkD-YLpqtUKKcx8kR9zXIPa8U8XEw66OfQqePJ4Q1Q/export?format=txt) separates authorization, app installation/repository selection and final connection. [AWS documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/dtconsole/latest/userguide/connections-create-github.html) independently distinguishes `PENDING` from `AVAILABLE`. This is guide/documentation provenance, not proof every step completed on video.
- **Choose review depth by failure visibility.** The [AI-code discussion, 4:12 to 6:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqXg5l-8VZY&t=252s), explains why plausible numerical outputs can conceal wrong implementation details, and why an engineer needs actionable knowledge of components/schema. Tests and implementation review are complementary when outputs cannot expose the relevant defect.
- **Evaluate only exercised capabilities.** The sponsored [Devin evaluation, 7:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLTM3HCzg0o&t=446s), explicitly withholds a stronger problem-solving rating because no truly ambiguous task was tested, and still requires merge review. Its [reported 50 GB SQLite growth and revoked pasted keys, 3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLTM3HCzg0o&t=193s), support checking runtime resource producers and secret remediation, not a universal reliability score.

## Adopted: scoped orchestration and domain learning

- **Project context and authority are separate.** Jimmy's [project-isolated lead agents, 10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q6NY3KbYCAs&t=643s), and [meeting-to-work approval, 11:35 to 12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q6NY3KbYCAs&t=695s), motivate explicit project ownership and candidate tasks with source/acceptance checks. Transcribed discussion is evidence to interpret, not new execution authority.
- **Learn unfamiliar workflows before replacing tools.** At [7:31 to 8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7cFuyTJEX1I&t=451s), he describes unknown operational details delaying custom dialer/email tooling. Use existing workflows to discover constraints before committing to bespoke automation. This does not endorse buying unknown software or copying a competitor's screens/features.

## Rejected or unresolved claims

- **Tutorial production/security labels are not proof.** Jan's [pinned API auth](https://github.com/ski043/api-yt/blob/89f0191380b8f841332ab543938914b08bc2542b/app/lib/auth.ts#L9-L35) is a fixed demo-token map; the [1:00:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xp4g7k8r1TE&t=3602s) memory limiter assumes a long-running process, not a global multi-instance quota. No vendor stack, sample tokens or public tutorial reads become production defaults.
- **Correct inaccurate database shorthand.** Contrary to the [53:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xq2fYRA3dj8&t=3185s) explanation, PostgreSQL [`NO ACTION`](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/ddl-constraints.html#DDL-CONSTRAINTS-FK) still enforces referential integrity and can reject deletion. PostgreSQL also does not require every table to declare a primary key, though its [documentation recommends one](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/ddl-constraints.html#DDL-CONSTRAINTS-PRIMARY-KEY).
- **Narration, diagrams and current code can disagree.** Jan's [pinned README](https://github.com/ski043/kinde-systems-design/blob/5580a86f26aeabdbc8dada153fc66186e12d4a2f/README.md) describes an older auth/no-billing design than the same commit's [v2 PRD](https://github.com/ski043/kinde-systems-design/blob/5580a86f26aeabdbc8dada153fc66186e12d4a2f/docs/PRD.md). Preserve contradictions; do not average them into an invented implementation.
- **Scoped identities/spend do not prove autonomous safety.** Jimmy's [11:04 to 11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q6NY3KbYCAs&t=664s) assurance conflicts with his own [16:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q6NY3KbYCAs&t=972s) red-team caveat. Identity separation does not establish defenses against injection, exfiltration or destructive actions. Autonomous sign-ups, 2FA handling and subscription reuse are not adopted authorization/licensing guidance. Devin's [“0%” leak assurance, 3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLTM3HCzg0o&t=193s), is likewise unsupported.
- **Speed, revenue and agent counts are anecdotes.** Jimmy's [efficiency claim, 6:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q6NY3KbYCAs&t=382s), and [parallelism/spending discussion, 12:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q6NY3KbYCAs&t=763s), supply no controlled accepted-work baseline. The AWS video's [“month in 90 minutes” conclusion, 1:17:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hOYzvUQKq8s&t=4622s), is not measured comparative productivity. Its [45:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hOYzvUQKq8s&t=2753s) discussion chooses dashboards/load testing over offered CI/CD setup; a complete pipeline rollout was not demonstrated. Sponsorship and sales claims are not independent engineering evidence.
