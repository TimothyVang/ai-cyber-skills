# Motion graphics: engines, stack and lessons

Detail behind `references/frontend/playbook/05a-motion-graphics.md`, from 16 repos and tools plus two creators' channels. Per-repo notes and demo clips: `TimothyVang/frontend:reference/github/motion-graphics/`; channel transcripts: `TimothyVang/frontend:reference/youtube/channels/`.

## Headline findings

1. **Every serious pipeline is the same machine:** a page or component whose every frame is a **pure function of `t`** (`render(t)`, `seek(t)`, `useCurrentFrame()`, a paused GSAP timeline), captured frame by frame in headless Chrome, with audio placed from **one timeline file** and muxed by ffmpeg at **−14 LUFS**. The frameworks (Remotion, HyperFrames, Diffusion Studio) differ in authoring surface and licence, not in this contract.
2. **Quality comes from the director's brief, not the engine.** Lukas got a weaker film from Codex + HyperFrames than from Claude's dependency-free HTML. All the strong prompts share one skeleton: inputs with defaults → direction with numbers and a **Banned** list → beat map → build rules → gotchas → a **gate before rendering** (show the beat map / stills first).
3. **Video motion is not UI motion.** UI rules cap motion at ~300 ms because users are waiting. In film, the viewer must *read* every beat once at full speed: entrances 400 ms, fast moves 0.3–0.6 s, landed titles held dead still ≥ 1 s, reading holds of 0.3 s/word + 0.5 s. Three sources independently report that first drafts run too fast (shotcraft: every note asked for "slower"; HyperFrames: "slowing the whole video by about a second often fixes it"; ClaudeAnimationBase: an ending first packed into 1.3 s that "nobody could tell what had happened").
4. **Real UI, never a mockup** is unanimous for launch films (HyperFrames, video-shotcraft, Diffusion Studio; MagicPath's no-device-mockups default): real screenshots and real components, with the film's visual language derived from the product's own tokens.

## Cross-source lessons

1. **Every frame is a pure function of `t`.** No `Date.now()`, unseeded random, CSS transitions, timers, `requestAnimationFrame` accumulation or integrated physics, so any frame renders alone and in parallel. — lemo-opuscar, hyperframes, remotion, ClaudeAnimationBase, shotcraft, awesome-opus
2. **One timeline file is the single source of truth** for picture, score, SFX, subtitles and checks; write times in beats or sections and round to frames only at the last step. — lemo-opuscar, librosa, hyperframes
3. **Plan in words before code, then gate.** Treatment / three storylines / STORYBOARD with reads / beat map, and show the beat map or 6 stills before rendering. Opus "defaults to the first route" (single HTML, zero deps) unless a framework is named. — hyperframes, lemo-opuscar, ClaudeAnimationBase, opus-video-prompts, remotion
4. **Turn adjectives into numbers and ban the defaults by name.** "premium" = a material with numbers; the BANNED list grows after every render. The most-banned items: glows, bouncy easing, particle bursts, template look, gradients on UI chrome, dead time, crossfades. — hyperframes, awesome-opus, opus-video-prompts
5. **Model the viewer: one read at a time, fast actions, slow meanings.** List each shot's reads, give each time to be found and understood, never overlap two important reads; the cause is seen before the reaction. — ClaudeAnimationBase, hyperframes
6. **First drafts are too fast, so budget holds first.** Every piece of shotcraft feedback said "slower"; a wordmark holds ≥ 1 s, 0.5 s after batch motion, the opening hero arc is ≥ 3 s; text holds 0.3 s/word + 0.5 s (last line ≥ 2 s) or `letters/15 + 1.5` s; title card ≥ 4 s; hold 0.3–0.75 s before the payoff. — shotcraft, hyperframes, lemo-opuscar, ClaudeAnimationBase
7. **Landed text is dead still; motion moves elsewhere.** No breathing, floating or creeping scale on titles or UI; idle wobble reads as "the video is waiting". Each phase owns a sustained-motion route instead (staged reveals, camera with intent, the UI working, cursor-led action). — hyperframes
8. **Every seam is a caused transition carried by an object; worlds never crossfade.** Use 3–5 kinds per film (zoom through a button, object becomes the world, morph, whip, iris from the click, colour flood); exit and entry match axis, direction and speed, and cut mid-motion. ClaudeAnimationBase: "Every seam gets a transition." — hyperframes, ClaudeAnimationBase, lemo-opuscar, diffusion
9. **Ease by role, not one curve.** Arrivals long-tailed (`expo.out`, `bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`), camera `inOut`, exits accelerate and run 25–50% shorter; springs by damping (0.7–0.85 premium, 1.0 for UI that mustn't overshoot), and opacity and colour never bounce. Let opacity finish before movement. Brand energy and tone pick the preset. — hyperframes, diffusion, shotcraft, remotion
10. **Beat sync governs timing, not amplitude.** Cut on the beat, change the layout on the bar, at most 3 full-frame impacts per film on the strongest hits (≥ 16 beats apart); per-beat motion stays on elements. Fit the grid; don't trust the tempo scalar. Verify cut error ≤ 3 frames on the rendered file and subtract the encoder's constant audio offset once. — librosa, shotcraft, hyperframes
11. **Sound is half the film and every effect has a visible cause.** Use three layers (ambience, foley, music), two real silences before the turn, sound as a transition (J/L-cut), riser→impact once, rotated takes and stepped volumes for repeats, cinematic timbres not game or meme sounds, a measured −14 LUFS. — lemo-opuscar, hyperframes, shotcraft, remotion
12. **Launch films show the real product doing one real task.** Establish the workspace → isolate the action at 1.6–2× → return the result; the cursor is 4–7% of the frame and its click fires the next beat on the same frame; never invent UI, never tilt product UI in 3D, never a device mockup. Results-based films out-performed effects-heavy ones about 10×. — hyperframes, shotcraft, diffusion, MagicPath
13. **The film's look grows from the product's tokens or from one medium,** never a promo skin: extract fonts, scale, radii and colours from the source; a look is "the rules of one medium" (paper, ink, glass) with its native moves; references teach grammar, "never its content, logos, or characters". — shotcraft, hyperframes, lemo-opuscar, MagicPath, `747ZnEtsRbg` 11:55
14. **Text in video has floors measured on the rendered frame.** Captions ≥ 56 px effective at 1080 (fontSize × scales × perspective), secondary ≥ 28–32 px, headline ≥ 84 px at 1080 wide; one or two text elements per shot; rewrite copy rather than shrink type; check every readable line at 360–480 px width. — shotcraft, hyperframes, remotion, diffusion
15. **Look at the motion, not the code, at three zoom levels,** then have a clean-context reviewer judge it. Contact sheet → 0.2 s or consecutive-frame strips around every key move and seam → full-resolution crops of faces and text → one full-speed viewing with sound. Then an independent subagent with only the storyboard and sheets. — ClaudeAnimationBase, lemo-opuscar, shotcraft, hyperframes, pixelfx
16. **Iterate in drafts, not one-shots.** Write 3–5 genuinely different drafts, look, fix the worst thing, at least 3 rounds; "so-called one-shots" usually ran two or more rounds; one fix round before delivery, then only the user's notes. — pixelfx, opus-video-prompts, lemo-opuscar
17. **Effects need a reason.** Glow marks information (the electron being discussed), one highlight per shot clipped to its bounds, DoF is atmosphere not clarity, physics where physics *is* the explanation; "remove a third of the motion" when it feels busy. — react-postprocessing, rapier, shotcraft, hyperframes
18. **Agent pipelines leak and spend:** telemetry beacons in skills, unauthenticated sockets running arbitrary Python, "always allow" paid MCPs, multimodal `listen` uploads, keys read from `.env`. Deny `.env` reads, keep sockets on localhost, use safe modes, scope permissions. — hyperframes, mcp-for-blender, fish-speech, diffusion, opus-video-prompts

## Contradictions between sources

| Topic | Side A | Side B | Our call |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critique loop | Lukas (`747ZnEtsRbg` 18:09): score each dimension, iterate until all are 7–8+ | HyperFrames `review.md`: "Never score the film out of 10: a score finds nothing to fix"; write `t=… · where · problem → change` notes, P0/P1/P2 | Notes with timestamps and severity (matches PLAYBOOK §2 P-levels); no numeric film scores |
| Music in code | Lemo-Opuscar and Lukas synthesise original scores in code on the picture's timeline | HyperFrames: "Do not spend the run building an audio chain or writing music in code: nobody can hear whether it is good" | Licensed or user-supplied track by default; synthesis only for stylised pieces (chip, drone, mechanical pulse) and always measured |
| SFX alignment | shotcraft/@twoclipping: the measured peak lands *on* the event frame | HyperFrames: the peak lands **one frame after** its picture ("light arrives before sound") | Peak at +1 frame; it's never perceived as early |
| Hard cuts | Lemo-Opuscar: "Avoid default fades and hard cuts"; ClaudeAnimationBase: transitions at every seam | Diffusion Studio: "Prefer a hard cut between layout forms"; HyperFrames: ≤ 1/3 of seams may be hard cuts; shotcraft `beat-cut-moves` uses hard cuts as a beat instrument | Hard cuts only on action, on the beat, or between layout forms; never as the default seam |
| Overshoot | Swiss Motion / @heyiamnick: critically damped, "nothing ever passes its target" | HyperFrames / shotcraft: `back.out(1.4–1.7)`, 1.04–1.12 overshoot by brand | Brand preset decides; product UI in our films uses no overshoot (PLAYBOOK §5 bounce rules) |
| Text in video | ClaudeAnimationBase: "No text… Show it, don't write it" | Launch films: titles carry the argument (HyperFrames, shotcraft C1: no silent stretch > 3 s) | Character pieces: no text; product films: titles per HyperFrames copy rules |
