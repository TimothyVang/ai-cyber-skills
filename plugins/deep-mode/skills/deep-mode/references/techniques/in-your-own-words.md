# in-your-own-words

**Use when:** starting a task, briefing a subagent, opening a PR `## Why` or final report, or asked to restate.

1. Read the user's message, the conversation, and any commits or PR description.
2. Restate in your own words what the user's goals are and what problem they're trying to solve; if the task ships a change, add the smallest change you expect to meet it.
   Done when the restatement names the goal, the problem, and (for a change) the smallest expected change.
3. Proceed without waiting for confirmation. If evidence rules out the requested change, show it and stop.

**Hard rule.** Your own words, not a paraphrase of theirs: goal and problem, not the task list.

**Done when** the restatement is written where it was needed (the task log, the brief, the PR `## Why`, or the report) and work has moved on or stopped on shown evidence.
