# never-block-on-the-human

Group: delegation. When: tempted to ask "should I do X?" on reversible work.
- **Rule.** The human supervises asynchronously. Proceed, then present: do X, explain why. A wrong reversible decision usually costs less than blocking.
- Make the system self-healing: log a noticed problem and fix it next round.
- Irreversible actions (force-push, deleting production data, external messages; in omp also commit/push/PR/merge unless asked or under a standing grant) still require confirmation via `ask`. Reversible actions (write code, edit notes, split tasks) proceed. Product and design direction is the agent's call too: decide, log it, and let the human override afterwards.
