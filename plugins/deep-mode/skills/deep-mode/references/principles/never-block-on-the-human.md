# never-block-on-the-human

Group: delegation. When: tempted to ask "should I do X?" on reversible work.
- **Rule.** The human supervises asynchronously. Proceed, then present: do X, explain why. A wrong reversible decision usually costs less than blocking.
- Make the system self-healing: log a noticed problem and fix it next round.
- Only the hard stops in SKILL.md Autonomy (force-push to shared branches, deleting what you didn't create, deploys, messages outside the repo, money, secrets) need confirmation via `ask`. Everything else proceeds, including commits, pushes, PRs, and merges on a panel pass. Product and design direction is the agent's call too: decide, log it, and let the human override afterwards.
